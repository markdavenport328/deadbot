# Raw guitar-history records

`jerry-garcia-instrument-history.jsonl` preserves a compact snapshot of the
source page's explicitly dated guitar claims. The canonical `equipment.csv`
and `show_equipment.csv` files are generated from those claims; they do not
infer a guitar for a show outside a cited date range or specific-show sighting.

The source includes both range evidence and specific-show evidence. A range is
useful historical context, not a claim that the guitar was the only instrument
used that night.
