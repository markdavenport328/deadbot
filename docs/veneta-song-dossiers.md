## Reading these entries

- A **catalog/credit page** is a reference link, not an independent scholarly claim.
- An **interview, oral history, or editorial note** is retained as attributed context; it should be presented as what its speaker or author says.
- A **Veneta performance link** applies to that one rendition and should not be generalized to every performance of the song.
