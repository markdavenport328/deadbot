   tracks and a play control and a note on what is on it. The facts check out

   described as two nights, February 13–14, 1970. None of the failure
   conditions apply. It did not call `get_grid`: the page is about a few
   records to start with, and album units with playable tracklists fit that.
2. **Who sat in.** One `search_guest_musicians` call, then two person
   rosters: 16 repeat guests and 28 notable visitors, each with show count,
   first and last year and instruments. The counts match the library (Ned
   Lagin 33, David Nelson 20, Bruce Hornsby 16, Hamza el-Din and John Dawson
   15), Hornsby appears once (the #65 merge holds), and the first and last
   years give the "when". None of the failure conditions apply. No grid. The
   roster's year span answers "who and roughly when". A guest × year grid
   would show the year-by-year shape (Ned Lagin's 23 shows in 1974 against 1
   to 5 in other years), which the roster flattens.

## What this says about the new pairs

- Both questions got correct, playable pages without a grid. The suite's
  failure conditions accept that, so that is a pass, not a miss.
- The new pairs are in the palette and the tool. Whether the model reaches
  for them on a question that needs them ("compare the 1972 Dick's Picks by
  their jams", "when was Ned Lagin around?") is untested. Those are the next
  live questions to try before changing any prompt text.
- Reaching a series of records takes a SQL listing first (36 IDs) before
  `get_grid` can be called. For show rows, a `tour` argument gathers a tour's
  nights. A matching way to select a series of records (all volumes whose
  title starts with a phrase) would make the record grid one call away. It is
  not built; it is listed in the task report as an option.
