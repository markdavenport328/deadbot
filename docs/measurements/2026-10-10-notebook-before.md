# Follow-up cost before the research notebook (2026-10-10)

Baseline for the research-notebook plan, taken on the branch with the grid tools merged and the notebook not yet wired into the API. The follow-up questions are asked as one conversation (the browser's visible transcript sent back each time), response cache off, in-process against the live model, store served from the local SQLite build. The run asked for the notebook but the API sent none, so the notebook columns are zero and each section is labeled "notebook requested, none sent". The tables and labels were regenerated from the saved raw metrics with the corrected labeling in `scripts/measure_turns.py` (no new model calls); the numbers are the original run's. Europe '72 ran first on a cold provider cache (cached share 0.433 on its first turn), 1979 second on a warmer one (0.848), so compare across sequences with that in mind.

What the numbers say:
- Follow-ups each pay for a fresh round of research: turns 2 and 3 take 2 to 3 model calls and 17,000 to 25,000 peak input tokens, with 4.5 to 12.5 seconds to the first answer (the Lyceum question is the slowest at 12.5 s).
- The first turn of Europe '72 had one truncated tool result (73,673 characters), which no later turn repeats.
- Cached input share runs 0.80 to 0.87 after the first turn of each conversation and 0.43 on the first turn of the run.
- The 1979 follow-ups are lighter than the Europe '72 ones (the third turn made 2 calls and answered in 4.5 s), so the Europe '72 path is the clearer baseline to beat.
