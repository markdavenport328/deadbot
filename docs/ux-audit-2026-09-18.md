# Interface audit and design pass — 2026-09-18

Scope: the interfaces plugin's `interface-review` skill run as a whole-repository
audit of `web/src/App.tsx` and `web/src/styles.css`, rendered against the
fixtures, followed by a design pass mocked against the Branford fixture. This
note records what was decided and what is still open so a future agent can
implement without re-deriving it. Nothing here is implemented yet except the
`font-synthesis` removal (2026-09-21).

## How to re-run the audit

- The skill is not in the Skill tool's list. Read it from
  `~/.claude/plugins/cache/interfaces/interfaces/<ver>/skills/interface-review/SKILL.md`,
  with `better-interface` and the six `better-*` domain skills beside it.
- `.claude/launch.json` is gitignored; add a `deadbot-web` entry
  (`npm run dev --prefix web -- --port 5199 --strictPort`) in any worktree.
- Fixtures: `/?fixture=branford|cornell|shakedown|blocks|evolution|album|performance|fact|legacy|views|songs`.
- Fixture mode short-circuits `askQuestion`, so submit, loading, focus return and
  screen-reader announcement cannot be exercised there.

## Design decisions from the pass (approved by Mark)

Not up for reconsideration. The palette stays as it is; it is derived from the
Veneta 1972 "Sunshine Daydream" cover (greens, gold, rose). Only the blue's hue
is open. Do not propose palette shifts or decorative background graphics; both
were tried and rejected.

### Type tiers

- One loud thing per page. The page h1 is it: `clamp(2.2rem, 3.4vw, 3.1rem)`,
  Fraunces with `"SOFT" 40, "opsz" 96`, tracking `-0.015em`, max-width 22ch.
- Body 1rem; lead paragraphs 1.125rem at line-height 1.6, max-width 62ch.
- Small tier 0.8rem. Nothing rendered below 12px. Today there are 35 font sizes,
  five under 12px (`.k` labels 10.9px, `.source-kind-chip` 9.6px).
- Card h2 drops from 2.1rem to about 1.6rem with tracking `-0.01em`. At present
  the card h2 (33.6px) overpowers the h1 (28px) below about 1200px wide.

### Large (primary) show cards

- Identity block is a three-line grid with 0.25rem gap: eyebrow label, then
  venue and date together on one line (cream, 600 weight, body size, wrapping
  as a flex row with 0.5rem gap), then the meta line. Do not highlight the date
  alone; the venue and date are a pair.
- Headline 1.25rem below the identity block.
- Note under the headline: max-width 58ch, body size, full text colour on
  primary cards.
- One generous break (2.75rem) used three times per card: before the listen
  zone, before the drawer tabs, before the footer. Inside a zone stay tight
  (0.5rem to 1rem).

### Small (supporting) show cards

- Identity line at the small tier, 500 weight, standard text colour (not cream).
  Meta in the quiet colour.
- Headline about 1.45rem with 1.4rem above it so identity and headline never
  read as one block.
- Note in the muted colour.

### Links

- Setlist song links and listen-for links carry a real underline:
  `text-decoration-color: rgba(231,185,78,0.45)` (0.55 for listen-for),
  `text-underline-offset: 0.16em`. Unlinked songs stay plain. This closes the
  audit's one HIGH finding (see below).
- Home example questions: text colour, 0.95rem, underline in
  `rgba(63,149,220,0.5)` with 0.18em offset.

### Rail and cards

- The rail has no background of its own and no right border. It sits on the
  page ground (`#14221b`) so it shares the canvas with the main panel. 300px
  wide, 0.9rem text, wordmark 1.35rem, sticky at top, full viewport height,
  padding `1.5rem 1.25rem 1.25rem`.
- Cards lift off the ground as a dark transparency: `rgba(4,10,6,0.38)` with a
  faint 1px top highlight and a soft shadow. The page should feel open, not
  boxed.

### Drawer tabs

- Tabs read as navigation. Row: `gap: 0 1.5rem`, 2.75rem above, one hairline
  rule beneath. Tab: muted colour, body size, 500 weight,
  `padding: 0.2rem 0 0.7rem`, `margin-bottom: -1px`. Open tab: cream text with a
  2px cream underline sitting on the hairline. Counts in the quiet colour with
  tabular numerals at the small tier.
- Use disclosure semantics (`aria-expanded` on buttons) rather than a
  `role=tablist` in which no tab is ever selected.

### Home screen

- Starts without the rail (`grid-template-columns: 0px minmax(0,1fr)`).
- Centred serif lead line, `clamp(1.6rem, 2.6vw, 2.2rem)`, 500 weight,
  max-width 34ch. Centred composer, max-width 42rem, textarea at 1.125rem with
  padding `1rem 1.1rem 3.4rem`, and an Explore button.
- Below: the five library numbers (shows, songs, performances, recordings,
  guests) in display-size serif, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`, tabular numerals,
  laid out `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(11rem, 1fr))` with `gap: 2.25rem 2rem`.
  Three example questions under each number. The Compare category is gone.
- The last two questions under performances and recordings in the mock were
  placeholders; pick real ones the catalog can answer.

### Transition on ask

Timings follow Emil Kowalski's animation rules (`npx skills@latest add
emilkowalski/skills`): transform and opacity only, never ease-in, exits fade
without stagger, reduced motion is gentler not zero.

- Easings: ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, ease-in-out
  `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`, drawer `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`.
- Questions and lead fade out over 180ms, no movement. The composer stays put.
- Rail slides in from `translateX(-100%)` over 450ms on the drawer easing; its
  contents fade in over 300ms after a 120ms delay. The main panel moves from
  `translateX(-300px)` to rest over 400ms on ease-in-out, delayed 120ms.
- Answer blocks appear with a 220ms ease-out fade starting at about 540ms,
  successive blocks 80ms apart.
- Sequence: add `leaving`; after 180ms hide the start screen, show the answer
  with `streaming`, add `opening`, remove `start`, force reflow, next frame
  swap `opening` for `arriving`; clean up after about 1300ms.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: keep opacity fades, drop the translates.
- Gate every hover style behind `@media (hover: hover)`.
- `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--listen); outline-offset: 2px; }`.

## Audit findings still open

Verdict on 2026-09-18 was Block, on the HIGH finding alone. Contrast was
measured for 28 colour pairs; every text tier passes AA on the flat ground.

HIGH

1. Links indistinguishable from static text at rest. Setlist link vs unlinked
   song is 1.62:1 colour-only; `.plain a` is styled identically to
   `.plain-title`; home starting-point buttons look like body text. Fixed by the
   underline decisions above.

MEDIUM

2. `font-synthesis: none` with no italic Fraunces or Inter face rendered every
   `<em>` upright. The rule was removed on 2026-09-21; synthesized italics now
   apply. If a real italic face is ever self-hosted, the rule can return.
3. The whole content pane is `aria-live`, so every render re-announces. Narrow
   the live region to the streaming answer, and announce completion once.
4. Tab pattern: `role=tablist` with `aria-selected` toggling to null (zero
   selected tabs), every tab `tabindex=0`, no arrow-key movement. Replace with
   disclosure buttons as decided above.
5. Flat heading outline: group `<h2>` and card `<h2>` sit at the same level
   (`App.tsx` group headings around lines 1533 and 1538; card headings at 863,
   868, 1021, 1184, 1236). Cards inside a group should be `<h3>`.
6. Card h2 overpowers h1 below about 1200px. Fixed by the type tiers above.
7. Focus is dropped on ask: textarea and submit are disabled while loading, so
   keyboard focus falls to `<body>`. Move focus to the answer heading, or keep
   the textarea focusable and mark it busy.
8. 35 distinct font sizes, five under 12px. Collapse to the tiers above.
9. Two golds in use (`#e7b94e` token and raw `#c49e48` on focus rings,
   numerals and the claim bar), and gold on chrome that is not a listen action.
   Colour means action: gold hears, rose reads, blue asks. Consolidate to one
   gold and take it off non-listen chrome.
10. Error strings ("Deadbot could not answer just now.", four sites in
    `App.tsx`) offer no next step. Say whether to retry, rephrase, or wait.

LOW and notes

11. No `color-scheme: dark` on `:root`, so form controls and scrollbars render
    light.
12. Hover styles are ungated and fire on touch.
13. Streaming answer is replaced wholesale on each chunk rather than appended,
    which contributes to the live-region noise in finding 3.
14. Group and claim text max-width 58rem is wider than comfortable reading;
    the new tiers cap lead text at 62ch and notes at 58ch.
15. `--r: 2px` corners, the six-zone card anatomy and the tabbed drawer are
    approved design-system decisions and are not findings; keep them.

## Not verified

Submit flow, loading state, focus return after an answer, and screen-reader
announcement of a finished answer. Fixture mode cannot exercise them; test
against a live API or add a fixture that replays a stream.
